## 1. Strategy — how it works
Actively managed ETF; under normal conditions ≥80% of assets in domestic and
foreign equities of companies tied to its theme. **No underlying index** — ARK's
team selects and re-weights holdings with daily discretion. Originally "Space
Exploration & Innovation"; **renamed in late 2025 to add "Defense Innovation,"**
broadening the mandate. Implication: returns depend heavily on ARK's stock
selection and conviction sizing, not on a transparent rulebook → **key-person /
manager risk** is a first-order factor here.

## 2. Theme purity ⭐
Holdings center on genuine space/defense names (Rocket Lab, L3Harris, Kratos,
Teradyne, plus some broad industrials/tech like Deere, AMD). Compared with a
passive peer, ARK's active picks tend to be **recognizably on-theme**, but the
**2025 mandate expansion to defense** means "space purity" is now intentionally
diluted with defense exposure.

industrial* blend, not pure space. Know you're buying ARK's definition of the theme.

## 4. Sector & geographic exposure
Heavily **Industrials (~60%)** and **Technology (~28%)**; primarily US with some
foreign. **Overlap watch:** big defense primes (LHX, etc.) also dominate XAR/SHLD —
holding ARKX *and* a defense ETF would double up, not diversify.

## 5. Cost in context
0.75% — top of the space cohort and you're also paying for active management.
Cheaper passive alternatives exist (ROKT 0.45%, UFO 0.75% passive). Justified
only if you believe ARK's active selection adds value net of the fee.
