# Theme: Space

The "space economy" — launch, satellites, earth-observation, comms, and the
defense names that ride alongside. A textbook **high-risk thematic** area: small
universe, young funds, lots of pre-profit companies, heavy concentration, and a
big gap between marketing and holdings.

This folder is the **worked pilot** for the whole framework.

> XAR and SHLD are deliberately included as **reference points**. A lot of "space"
> exposure is really aerospace/defense; comparing against the pure A&D funds shows
> how much extra (and how much overlap) you actually get from a space label.

## Key tension in this theme

1. **"Space" vs "defense" drift.** Several space funds broadened mandates toward
   defense in 2025 (ARKX literally renamed to "Space *& Defense* Innovation").
   The pure-space bet and the defense bet are different risk profiles.
2. **Concentration.** These funds run ~34–54 holdings with top-10 weights near
   55–60%. You're making a fairly concentrated bet either way.
3. **Cost.** Space funds cluster at 0.75%; the broad A&D funds are 0.35–0.50% for
   arguably more liquidity and diversification. Decide if pure-space access is
   worth the premium.
4. **Data quality.** Aggregator figures here disagreed with SEC filings (see
   `comparison.md` notes). Cross-check before deciding.
