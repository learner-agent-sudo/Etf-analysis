## Discrepancies & data-quality notes (the reason this framework exists)

- **UFO top-10 concentration:** an aggregator reported **~57.7%**, but UFO's
  **2023 SEC N-CSR filing** implied **~22.3%** for its then-top-10. By **May 2026**
  the live top-10 summed to roughly **~50%**. → Concentration is both *time-varying*
  and *source-dependent*; always date it and prefer the filing/fact sheet.
- **UFO holdings character changed over time:** the 2023 filing listed names like
  Nitori (furniture) and Xylem (water tech) — weak theme purity. By May 2026 the
  top names were Rocket Lab, Planet Labs, ViaSat, Globalstar, Iridium, Intuitive
  Machines — strong purity. → "Theme purity" is not static; re-check at decision time.
- **ARKX AUM:** sources ranged from **~$500M to ~$919M**. Verify on the fact sheet.
- **ARKX identity:** renamed to include **"Defense"** in late 2025 and broadened its
  mandate — a textbook **theme-drift** event. The fund you research today is not
  the fund that launched in 2021.
